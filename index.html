Welcome to the Advisory Portal

System Overview

Welcome to your AI-powered smart irrigation hub. Our system analyzes farm data, soil moisture status, and localized weather forecasts to deliver precise, actionable irrigation recommendations tailored to your crops.

Benefits for Farmers & Officers

Optimize your water usage, enhance crop yields, and receive real-time advisory services directly from agricultural researchers and sugar factory management.

How the Advisory Works

  1. Collect: Field sensors send soil moisture, temperature, and weather readings.
  2. Analyze: The advisory engine compares sensor data with crop water requirements.
  3. Advise: Farmers receive irrigate, wait, or reduce-water recommendations on the dashboard.
