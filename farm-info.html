Crop Stages

Water requirements vary across different lifecycle stages of the crop:

Germination
Initial seed sprouting phase requiring precise, low-volume moisture control.
Tillering
Development of side shoots where controlled water management prevents root saturation.
Grand Growth
Rapid vegetative development period requiring peak irrigation support.
Maturity
Ripening phase where irrigation is gradually reduced to prepare for harvest.
